Face Landmarker model bundle: Google MediaPipe, float16/version 1, unmodified.
Source and SHA-256 are recorded in manifest.json.
Model documentation and model cards:
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker#models

The WASM runtime files are copied unmodified from the pinned npm distribution
during development/build preparation. The worker bundles the upstream JavaScript
runtime with Story Studio's worker adapter. All runtime/model resources load
locally. No camera frames are sent to Google or another network service.
