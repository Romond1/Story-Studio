# Card V2: external product evidence

Research date / retrieval date: **2026-10-07**. Scope: a live, teacher-controlled story lesson app with separate **Control** and **Present** surfaces. Research only; no application or episode changes.

## Method and confidence

- Sources below are official help, official product documentation/blogs, and one official open-source schema. Vendor descriptions establish available behavior, not independently measured learning outcomes.
- **Documented** means the cited source explicitly supports the behavior. **Assessment** means our UX/pedagogical interpretation. **Inference** means a possible Story Studio design lesson, not a claim about a vendor's private implementation.
- **Unknown** means not established by the sources reviewed; it does not mean unavailable. In particular, neither a responsive screenshot nor a character limit proves an automatic text-fit algorithm, minimum font, or clipping policy.
- This is documentation research, not authenticated hands-on testing. Plan restrictions and different desktop/mobile versions may matter. Sources were retrieved on the date above; a current crawl does not make an older article newly authored.
- No private database, synchronization protocol, or content-instance architecture is inferred as fact from product UI. Anki's documented conceptual model and H5P's published schema are explicitly identified exceptions where the model itself is public.

**Assessment for Card V2:** box geometry, preferred type size, actual rendered type size, content density and editor zoom should be distinct concepts. Choose a bounded fit policy with a readable floor, detect overflow, and offer a larger layout or split/rewrite action. Never infer that “the text is inside the rectangle” means students can read it. Long English, Japanese, mixed scripts, unbroken URLs, two-sided cards, images plus captions, and maximum-density grids need actual presentation-size checks.

## Five strongest transferable patterns

1. **Semantic content with compatible teaching treatments.** Anki is the explicit content/template reference; Wordwall is the practical reuse reference. For Card V2, a prompt, answer, hint, teacher note and media should retain their meaning when the teacher switches from explanation to reveal or matching. Conversion must validate required fields and explain incompatible targets. This recommendation is an inference from [Anki's documented model](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/getting-started) and [Wordwall's compatibility limits](https://wordwall.net/pc/features), not evidence that either has Story Studio's desired architecture.

2. **A genuinely private Control surface.** Pear Deck's dashboard establishes inspect-before-show, private scrolling and deliberate response sharing; Canva and PowerPoint establish notes/upcoming context separate from audience output. Card V2 should display the exact live audience state plus private preparation controls. An editor selection, property-panel opening or hover should not accidentally become a classroom action. [Pear Deck Dashboard](https://help.peardeck.com/migration/en/the-teacher-dashboard), [Canva modes](https://www.canva.com/help/presentation-modes/).

3. **Small, reversible teaching actions.** Genially's reveal/hide, Baamboozle's teacher-judged answers, and Pear Deck's show/hide/lock favor explicit verbs. Card V2 should make reveal, hide, reset, resolve and focus understandable without requiring teachers to assemble interaction graphs. Oral discussion should remain a first-class path. [Genially reveal](https://help.genially.com/how-to-set-up-reveal-interactivity-in-genially-B1LqcwHhi), [Baamboozle play instructions](https://www.baamboozle.com/game/1676072).

4. **Batch authoring with a reviewable mapping.** Quizlet's row→pair import, Anki field mapping and Canva's placeholder/row preview show three levels of complexity. Start Card V2 with a simple table/paste workflow, explicit column mapping, validation and per-row preview; keep media attachment usable without raw HTML. Reuse should allow a deliberate independent copy versus linked content policy rather than an unexplained duplicate icon. The linked/copy proposal is ours. [Quizlet import](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029977151-Creating-sets-by-importing-content), [Canva bulk](https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create/).

5. **Presentation fit and rehearsal are product features.** Google/PPT expose fit policies, H5P makes text-scaling versus container-growth explicit, and Kahoot/Mentimeter preview both sides. Card V2 should preview the selected layout with real content at presentation dimensions, show overflow before class, and rehearse reveal/reset without persisting lesson progress. Auto-shrink needs a floor and alternatives. [Slides fitting](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/10364036?hl=en-IN), [H5P schema](https://github.com/h5p/h5p-dialogcards/blob/master/semantics.json), [Kahoot preview](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/29968568026131-How-to-preview-and-test-your-kahoot-before-hosting-live).

## Patterns to reject or defer

- **A mandatory game loop for every card.** Timers, scores, leaderboards and join codes serve some activities; they should not interrupt a story prompt, picture discussion or teacher explanation. Keep an optional activity layer rather than making every item a quiz question.
- **A freeform slide editor as the default answer to flexibility.** Genially/Canva show its power, but unrestricted position, layering and wiring move work onto teachers. Prefer a few robust layouts with bounded resize behavior; allow advanced layout only when actual use cases justify it.
- **Universal “change type” conversion.** Wordwall explicitly documents incompatible content shapes. Silent dropping of answers, media, hints or distractors would be worse than refusing a conversion with a clear explanation.
- **Shrink forever or silently clip.** PowerPoint/Slides fit modes solve geometry, not a minimum readable size. No sampled edtech help establishes a universal classroom-safe text algorithm. Do not sell a character count as a fit guarantee.
- **Flatten imports without labeling the cost.** Wayground PDF imports are image slides; Kahoot import does not preserve audio/video/animations. Image-only material can be a useful fallback but should be labeled and should not masquerade as editable semantic card content.
- **Automatic propagation of every edit into a running lesson.** Canva and PowerPoint expose live-update choices. Card V2 needs an intentional rule for edits during presentation, especially when editing answers/reveals. “Apply to live session” or a stated snapshot policy is safer for teacher predictability than undocumented timing.
- **Anki's scheduler and template programming in the core live flow.** The useful transferable idea is semantic reuse. Individual scheduling and HTML/CSS editing do not follow from this lesson app's needs.
- **A large activity marketplace before core authoring works.** Nearpod/Pear Deck breadth is a later-stage reference. Card V2 should first make insertion, duplication, batch editing, media replacement, fit, reveal and reset reliable.

## Currentness notes and unresolved validation

1. **Nearpod import articles conflict over time.** The [June 2024 conversion guide](https://nearpod.com/blog/easily-convert-your-google-slides-powerpoint-presentations-into-interactive-nearpod-lessons-with-nearpodize/) describes static slide uploads; the [current update log](https://nearpod.com/blog/nearpod-updates/) states editable PPTX text/images/video layers were introduced February 2025. Use the later, more specific statement for PPTX. Do not generalize it to all import formats.
2. **Wayground naming is transitional.** Current documentation uses Presentation (formerly Lesson), while live-session docs still say Lesson. Treat these as documented terminology in transition, not proof of two separate products.
3. **Pear Deck's media-extension statement needs hands-on validation.** It is present in the current official article, but browser/account/platform differences could matter. The research does not claim the extension works in every 2026 environment.
4. **H5P schema is live `master`, not a pinned installed release.** The fit setting and data structure are directly observed published source. Deployment-specific defaults, rendering and bugs require version-pinned validation.
5. **Baamboozle evidence has two grades.** The vendor beginner guide documents workflow; public rendered editor/game pages supply visible control labels. These do not establish paid entitlement, disabled controls, server behavior, or full editing success.
6. **Text fit remains the largest evidence gap.** Before adopting any vendor-like behavior, test long and short prompts, long answers, Japanese/Latin mixtures, bold text, forced breaks, missing media, portrait/landscape media and dense multi-card layouts in actual Control/Present sizes.
7. **Copy is not link.** With the explicit Anki note/card model aside, duplication, importing and “reusable template” UI do not prove linked instances or shared editing propagation. Card V2 must choose and document its own reference/copy semantics.
